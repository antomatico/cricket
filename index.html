About these numbers

Games included. Every completed game with a scorecard. Left out: the 24 Jan forfeit win (no scorecard), the cancelled 29 Nov game, and 7 and 14 Mar, which PlayHQ still lists as in progress / pending.

Wickets. What PlayHQ credits each bowler. In this junior format a dismissed batter usually keeps batting, so team scores show few or no wickets even when bowlers took several.

Extras. Wides, no-balls, byes, leg-byes and penalty runs, as recorded on each scorecard.

Players are shown by initials: first name, surname, then the second letter of the surname (e.g. ECo).

Fill-in. Stand-in players appear in team totals but don't have a player card.

Strike rate is runs per 100 balls faced; economy is runs conceded per six-ball over.
